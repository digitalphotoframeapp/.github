# Digital Photo Frame App

**Turn your iPad, iPhone, Mac or Apple TV into a digital photo frame.** Play slideshows from your photo library, iCloud shared albums and Google Photos, with music.

[Website](https://digitalphotoframeapp.com) · [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/digital-photo-frame-with-music/id1219786089) · [Blog](https://digitalphotoframeapp.com/blog/) · [Support](https://digitalphotoframeapp.com/help/)

## What it does

- Photo slideshows with a clock and music
- Photos from the device, iCloud shared albums and Google Photos
- Runs on iPad, iPhone, Mac and Apple TV
- Made to stay on all day as a picture frame
